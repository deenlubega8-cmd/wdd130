Uganda

Uganda is a country found in East Africa. It's capital City is Kampala. It is a land locked country and its main economic activity is farming. it has neighboring countries in different directions; Sudan in North, Kenya in East, D.R congo in west, Tanzania in south and Rwanda in south-west. People in Uganda are called Ugandans and they speak English as their official language.
